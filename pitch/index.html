What it is

A side-view castle siege. Your castle on the left, the Black Legion on the right, one ground lane and the sky above it.

You are the Commander on the castle wall. Point and click to loose arrows with real physics. Your soldiers march, clash and die on their own. Recruit more with gold, or pick off the enemy that matters.

  • Destroy the enemy castle and you win on the spot.
  • Lose your castle and you do not lose the run. Reinforcements stop and the Commander is forced onto the field for a last stand.
  • The only way to lose is the Commander falling. No passive healing, so protect them.
  • Gold carries over between stages. No timers, no skill trees to manage, one currency.

In the game

Features

POINT-AND-CLICK ARCHERY

Manual physics. Point where you want the arrow to land and click. Standard arrows never run out.

HOLD TO FIRE

Hold the mouse button and keep shooting. Headshots kill instantly and chain into streaks.

THE BOW LEVELS UP

Your own kills raise your bow level, with a level-up flash and a call from your herald. Fire rate climbs from about one shot a second to about three.

FIVE SPECIAL ARROWS

Fire, ice, pierce, explosive and lightning. Each has its own cooldown, independent of the others.

POWER-UP BUBBLES

Shoot floating bubbles mid-battle for battle horns, healing, gold, rapid fire and free cavalry. A few are traps.

BATS AND BALLISTAS

Bat swarms bomb your line from the air. Siege ballistas on both sides lob flaming rocks at castles from across the map.

RECRUIT ON THE FLY

Seven unit slots, one tap each, always on screen. Small free waves march out on their own; buy more with gold.

BLACK KING BOSS

Stage 10. A giant boss and a last stand to win. Ten stages, one chapter, start to finish.
